Quick Tips for Answering

Use specifics: libs (Resilience4j, Spring Cloud Gateway, OpenTelemetry), patterns (outbox, saga), and numbers (timeouts, concurrency sizes).
Tie design choices to SLOs, error budgets, and business constraints.
Show trade-offs and migration paths, not only the happy path.
